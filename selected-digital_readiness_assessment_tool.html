Diagnostic Methodology

This diagnostic instrument maps organizational readiness criteria using validated operational parameters:

  • The 70% Principle: Establishes that primary transformation value is determined by human conditions surrounding the rollout—including governance, integration workflows, and culture—rather than the code model itself.
  • GSA IT Modernization Center of Excellence (C3): Evaluates alignment of Culture, Conditions, and Capabilities to verify standard technology scalability.
  • Prosci Maturity Framework: Categorizes technological integration status into 5 progressive stages, mapping transition milestones from reactive silos to integrated agile systems.
  • Governance Trust Standards: Outlines trust parameters and psychological safety milestones necessary to control modern autonomous digital agents.
